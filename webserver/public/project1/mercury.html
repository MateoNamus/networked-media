Mercury

Fun Facts About Mercury!

Mercury is the smallest planet in our solar system and the nearest to the Sun. It’s only slightly larger than Earth’s Moon.

From the surface of Mercury, the Sun would appear more than three times as large as when viewed from Earth, and sunlight would be up to seven times brighter.

Because Mercury is so close to the Sun, daytime temperatures can reach highs of about 800°F (430°C). But without an atmosphere to hold onto heat, nighttime temperatures can dip as low as −290°F (−180°C).

Mercury orbits the Sun in only 88 Earth days, making its “year” very short compared to Earth’s.

One day on Mercury (one full rotation) takes about 59 Earth days.

Mercury has a large iron core that makes up a big part of its interior, giving it a very high density for its size.

Despite its proximity to the Sun, there is water ice in permanently shadowed craters near Mercury’s poles.
