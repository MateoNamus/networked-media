Jupiter

Fun Facts About Jupiter!

Jupiter is the largest planet in our solar system, so big that all the other planets could fit inside it.

It is mostly made of hydrogen and helium, similar to a star, and it has no solid surface.

Jupiter’s iconic Great Red Spot is a giant storm that has been raging for at least 350 years and is so large that Earth could fit inside it.

The planet has at least 95 moons, with four major ones, Io, Europa, Ganymede, and Callisto, known as the Galilean moons.

Ganymede, one of Jupiter’s moons, is the largest moon in the solar system and is even bigger than the planet Mercury.

A day on Jupiter is the shortest of all the planets, lasting only about 10 Earth hours due to its rapid rotation.

Jupiter has a faint ring system made mostly of dust particles, discovered in 1979 by the Voyager 1 spacecraft.
