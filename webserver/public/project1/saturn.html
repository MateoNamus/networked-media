Saturn

Fun Facts About Saturn!

Saturn is the second-largest planet in our solar system and is famous for its stunning ring system, which is made mostly of ice and rock particles.

It is a gas giant composed primarily of hydrogen and helium, with no solid surface.

Saturn has at least 146 moons, with Titan being the largest. Titan is bigger than the planet Mercury and has lakes and rivers of liquid methane and ethane.

Despite its massive size, Saturn is the least dense planet in the solar system, it would float in water if a bathtub big enough existed.

One day on Saturn lasts about 10.7 Earth hours, while it takes about 29.5 Earth years to orbit the Sun.

Winds on Saturn can reach speeds of up to 1,100 miles per hour (1,770 kilometers per hour).

Saturn’s rings are divided into seven main groups, named alphabetically in the order they were discovered.
