Venus

Fun Facts About Venus!

Venus is the second planet from the Sun and our closest planetary neighbor. It is often called Earth’s “sister planet.”

It spins in the opposite direction from most planets (a retrograde rotation), and one full rotation on its axis takes about 243 Earth days, longer than its year, which is about 225 Earth days.

Venus has the hottest surface temperature of any planet in our solar system, hot enough to melt lead, because its thick atmosphere traps heat in a runaway greenhouse effect.

The atmosphere of Venus is extremely dense and is made up mostly of carbon dioxide (about 96.5%) with clouds of sulfuric acid.

The pressure at Venus’s surface is about 92-93 times the pressure at Earth’s sea level, similar to the pressure found nearly 1 km underwater on Earth.

Despite the intense heat and pressure, there is evidence of volcanoes and possibly ongoing volcanic activity on Venus.
