Fun Facts About Uranus!

Uranus is the seventh planet from the Sun and is known as an “ice giant” because of the icy materials, water, methane, and ammonia, that make up much of its interior.

It is unique because it rotates on its side, with an axial tilt of about 98 degrees. This means it essentially rolls around the Sun.

One year on Uranus takes about 84 Earth years, while one day lasts about 17 Earth hours.

The pale blue-green color of Uranus comes from methane gas in its atmosphere, which absorbs red light and reflects blue-green light.

Uranus has 27 known moons, most of which are named after characters from the works of William Shakespeare and Alexander Pope.

It also has a faint ring system, discovered in 1977, made of dark and narrow bands of particles.

Uranus is the coldest planet in the solar system, with minimum temperatures dropping to about -371°F (-224°C).
