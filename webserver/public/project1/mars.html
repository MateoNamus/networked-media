Mars

Fun Facts About Mars!

Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun and is often called the “Red Planet” because of its iron oxide rich soil, which gives it a reddish appearance.

Mars has the largest volcano in the solar system, Olympus Mons, which is about three times taller than Mount Everest.

It also has the deepest and longest canyon, Valles Marineris, which stretches more than 2,500 miles (4,000 km).

A day on Mars, known as a sol, is just a little over 24 hours (24 hours and 37 minutes).

Mars has two small moons, Phobos and Deimos, which are thought to be captured asteroids.

The thin atmosphere of Mars is made mostly of carbon dioxide, with very little oxygen, making it hostile for human life without support systems.

Evidence suggests that liquid water once flowed on Mars, and scientists continue to search for signs of past or present life there.
