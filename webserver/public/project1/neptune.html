Neptune

Fun Facts About Neptune!

Neptune is the eighth and farthest planet from the Sun in our solar system.

It is an ice giant, composed mostly of hydrogen, helium, and icy materials like water, methane, and ammonia.

Neptune has the strongest winds in the solar system, with speeds reaching up to 1,200 miles per hour (2,000 kilometers per hour).

One year on Neptune lasts 165 Earth years, while one day is about 16 hours long.

The planet has a deep blue color due to methane in its atmosphere, which absorbs red light and reflects blue light.

Neptune has 14 known moons, the largest of which is Triton. Triton orbits in the opposite direction of Neptune’s rotation and is thought to be a captured Kuiper Belt object.

Neptune also has a faint ring system, made up of dust and small rocks, which was confirmed by the Voyager 2 spacecraft in 1989.
