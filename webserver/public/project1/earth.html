Fun Facts About Earth!

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only known planet to support life.

About 71% of Earth’s surface is covered by water, with the remaining 29% consisting of continents and islands.

Earth has a protective atmosphere composed mainly of nitrogen (78%) and oxygen (21%), which shields life from harmful solar radiation and helps regulate temperature.

Earth’s rotation is gradually slowing, about 17 milliseconds per hundred years, making days slightly longer over time.

It takes Earth 365.25 days to orbit the Sun, which is why we add a leap day every four years to keep our calendars aligned.

The Earth’s core is as hot as the Sun’s surface, reaching temperatures of around 5,500°C (9,932°F).

Earth has one natural satellite, the Moon, which helps stabilize the planet’s tilt and creates tides in the oceans.
