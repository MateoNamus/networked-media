The Sun

Fun Facts About the Sun!

The Sun is about 4.5 billion years old and is considered to be a middle-aged “yellow dwarf” star.

It’s roughly 865,000 miles (1.39 million kilometers) in diameter, which means about 109 Earths laid side by side could span it.

The Sun contains about 99.86% of the mass of our entire solar system.

Light from the Sun takes about 8 minutes and 20 seconds to reach Earth.

The Sun doesn’t have a solid surface. What we see as the “surface” is the photosphere, which is a layer of hot plasma.

Its core is extremely hot, around 15 million degrees Celsius (about 27 million degrees Fahrenheit), which is where nuclear fusion happens.

The Sun’s outer atmosphere (the corona) is much hotter than its surface, reaching temperatures of millions of degrees.
